Ocean Enterprise Vocabulary

Terms used in the DCAT representation of Ocean Enterprise assets that are not covered by DCAT-AP or GeoDCAT-AP: blockchain, datatoken, compute-to-data, access-policy and pricing metadata.

Usage

Ocean Enterprise nodes publish asset metadata as DCAT-AP / GeoDCAT-AP JSON-LD at /api/aquarius/assets/dcat/<did>. Standard DCAT terms are used wherever they exist; this vocabulary covers the remaining Ocean Enterprise specific fields. Declare the prefix in your JSON-LD context:

{
  "@context": {
    "oec": "https://oceanenterprise.io/vocab/"
  }
}
